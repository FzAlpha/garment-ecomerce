Eleven pairs
of hands

Behind a courtyard on Rue de Saintonge, eleven artisans work at a pace set by the cloth, not the calendar.

One pattern, drafted by hand. One cutter per garment. One finisher who signs the label.

The process

Sourcing

We buy cloth by the bolt from a handful of mills we have worked with for decades, and every fibre can be traced to its farm.

Pattern

Each pattern is drafted by hand in paper, then refined across three toiles until the line falls exactly as intended.

Construction

Half-canvassed fronts, hand-set sleeves and over two hundred hand stitches in every collar.

Finishing

Pressed, inspected and signed by the artisan who made it — then repaired by them whenever it needs it.

“A coat should take longer to make than it takes to forget.”

Hélène Vérane
